## Purpose and justification
Buyers of payment platforms must choose between slow in-request fraud checks and risky after-the-fact checks. SentinelPay lets them choose by configuration, with reliable event-driven processing and auditable AI agents. The business aim is a product that can be sold to regulated customers and pay back its build cost.

## Objectives (SMART)
1. Deliver a releasable v1.0 by **week 26** (assumed 30 April 2027, from a 2 November 2026 start).
2. Complete the build within the **$703,175 baseline** (BAC), with a **$35,159 management reserve** held by the sponsor; total funding $738,334.
3. Meet the non-functional targets in document 02, with no open high or critical security findings.
4. Ship customer documentation and a compliance evidence pack at go-live.
5. Achieve payback by month 18 in the base case (document 06). This is a planning target, not a forecast.

## High-level scope
In: client, backend, agent engine, failover infrastructure, security and compliance readiness, documentation, pilot. Out: card-network certification, issuing, marketing campaigns. Detail in document 04.

## Milestones
Foundations (week 4), core payment flow (week 8), risk and agents (week 13), client and ops tooling (week 17), security and compliance freeze (week 22), release candidate (week 24), go-live (week 26).

## Summary budget
Labor $536,250; other direct costs $103,000; contingency $63,925; reserve $35,159 (document 06).

## Key stakeholders
Sponsor, product owner, engineering lead, security and compliance lead, pilot customers, gateway and cloud vendors, auditors, regulators (indirect). See document 10.

## High-level risks
Agent Framework preview API changes; model latency or cost; security findings late in the schedule; pilot customer availability; compliance scope creep; key-person loss. See document 07.

## Assumptions and constraints
Team of about 9 roles at the loaded rates in the cost model; cloud credits or budget available; sponsor decisions within 3 working days; scope changes through change control.

## Authority
The program manager approves day-to-day decisions within the baseline. Scope, schedule changes over **1 week**, or spend over **$10,000** outside the baseline go to the sponsor. Use of the management reserve needs sponsor approval.
